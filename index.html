2FA Generator

Paste a Base32 secret or an otpauth:// URI. Get the current 6-digit TOTP code and the next window. Compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, and any RFC 6238 SHA1 6/30 authenticator.

Privacy

  • Runs in the browser. The secret is never uploaded, logged, or written to storage.
  • SHA1 / 6 digits / 30 seconds only. Other otpauth settings are rejected so a plausible wrong code is never shown.
  • A ?secret= query is stripped on load. Do not put a TOTP secret in a shareable link.

Spaces and dashes are ignored. otpauth URIs that ask for 8 digits, SHA256, or a period other than 30 seconds are rejected.
